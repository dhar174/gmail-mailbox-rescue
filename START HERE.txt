MAILBOX RESCUE
================================================================================
Quick Start Guide for Preserving Your Work Gmail
================================================================================

Mailbox Rescue copies your work Gmail directly to your computer as standard,
portable files (.eml and .mbox) that you can open or import anytime.

Your data remains 100% private and stays entirely on your local machine.


HOW TO RUN AN EXPORT:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click "Mailbox Rescue.exe".
   * Note for Windows: If a "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen) warning
     appears, click "More info" and then click "Run anyway".

2. Click "Connect Google Account".
   * Your web browser will open to Google's sign-in page.
   * Sign in with the work account whose emails you want to preserve and approve
     read-only access.

3. Choose what to preserve:
   * "All Mail" (Recommended) preserves all messages (excluding Spam and Trash).
   * "Inbox only" preserves only your inbox messages.

4. Select a Destination Folder:
   * Click "Browse..." and choose an empty or dedicated backup folder.
   * IMPORTANT: Ensure the chosen drive has enough free disk space for your
     entire mailbox (often several gigabytes).

5. Click "Start Export".
   * Leave Mailbox Rescue open while it downloads and verifies your email.
   * The progress bar will show current scan and download status.


INTERRUPTIONS & RESUMING:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If your computer goes to sleep, restarts, or you close the app during export:
1. Reopen "Mailbox Rescue.exe".
2. Connect your account.
3. Select the same scope and the SAME destination folder.
4. Click "Start Export". Mailbox Rescue will safely resume where it left off,
   skipping already verified messages without creating duplicates.


WHAT TO KEEP:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When export completes, keep the entire backup folder intact. It contains:
  - messages/          Individual .eml files (can be opened in Outlook/Thunderbird)
  - mailbox.mbox       Standard mailbox archive
  - export.sqlite3     Export index and resume checkpoint database
  - export-report.html Verification and summary report (open in any web browser)
  - checksums.sha256   Cryptographic integrity hashes for all files
================================================================================
